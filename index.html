About BRIL

Brilliant Institute of Engineering & Technology

Hyderabad’s UGC Autonomous, NAAC ‘A’ Grade Engineering Institution — Est. 2008

Established in 2008, Brilliant Institute of Engineering & Technology (BRIL) has emerged as a Top Engineering College in Telangana, known for academic excellence, innovation, and industry-oriented education. With a vibrant student strength of over 6,000+ and a highly qualified faculty team, BRIL is committed to delivering quality technical education and holistic development.

BRIL is a proud UGC Autonomous Institution, accredited with NAAC 'A' Grade, ISO Certified, and NIRF Listed. The institute focuses on cutting-edge programmes, research-driven learning, industrial training, and skill development to nurture competent engineers with strong ethical values.

The Research & Development Cell was formally constituted, creating a structured framework for interdisciplinary research, funded projects, and industry collaboration.

2020
First Scopus & IEEE Publications

Faculty and students began publishing in SCI, Scopus, and IEEE-indexed journals — establishing BRIL's presence in the global research community.

2021
IPR Cell & First Patents

The Intellectual Property Rights Cell was launched. Faculty and students filed their first patents, protecting innovations in embedded systems, AI, and civil engineering.

2023
Doctoral Research Programme

Faculty began guiding Ph.D scholars in optical systems, AI, embedded systems, transportation engineering, and management — now 40+ active scholars.

2024
Industry-Sponsored Projects

Consultancy and sponsored research projects signed with HMDA, NHAI, Infosys, and private sector organisations — bridging academia and real-world problem-solving.

2025
Education Change Maker Award

Vice-Chairman Sri Kasireddy Durga Prasad Reddy conferred the prestigious Education Change Maker Award at Bridge'25 by ICT Academy — recognising BRIL's innovation-led leadership.
