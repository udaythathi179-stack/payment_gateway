Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay using PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm directly from my phone?

Yes! Our portal supports instant UPI intent. When using a mobile device, clicking on PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm will directly launch the app. On desktop or laptop, you can simply scan the dynamic on-screen QR code with any UPI app.

How does this replace the offline SBI Challan for Exam Transcripts?

Previously, students had to visit an SBI branch to deposit ₹60 per transcript copy. Now, select "Exam & Transcript Fee" on this portal, enter the number of copies, and pay ₹60 via PhonePe/GPay/Paytm. Print the generated e-Receipt and attach it to your transcript application form.

What happens if the money is debited but the receipt is not generated?

Every transaction has a unique gateway reference. In rare network interruptions, click the "Find Past Receipt" button in the top navigation and enter your Roll Number or Phone Number to fetch your verified payment slip.

Can educational loans or Net Banking be used for tuition fee?

Yes. The gateway accepts Net Banking across 50+ major banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Union Bank, etc.) as well as RuPay, Visa, and MasterCard debit/credit cards.
